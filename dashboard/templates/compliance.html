Compliance & risk

Persisted compliance verdicts and risk scores, with the method recorded for each assessment. Nothing on this page is generated by the dashboard; rows are read directly from Supabase.

How to read these assessments

Every row comes from the compliance_checks table. The method column identifies how the verdict was produced. Current rows use groq_llm_vector, written by the standalone comparison engine's Groq call; they were not produced by the queue pipeline. Model-reported confidence is not an accuracy guarantee and has not been independently validated.

Risk scores live in a separate risk_scores table. In this schema they are recorded against a company and category, not linked to a compliance check, so the dashboard does not imply a one-to-one relationship between the two tables.
